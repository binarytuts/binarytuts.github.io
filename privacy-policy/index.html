Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 26, 2026

This policy explains how the Android apps published by BinaryTuts on Google Play (the “apps”) handle information. It applies to every app listed on our home page.

Summary

  • We do not ask you to create an account and we do not collect your name, email address, phone number or contacts.
  • All app content is built in and works offline. Nothing you read is sent to us.
  • The apps show banner ads from Google AdMob. Google may collect some device information to show and measure ads, as described below.

Information stored on your device

The apps save a few settings on your device so they are remembered next time, such as the language you chose and, in device guides, the device model you selected. These settings stay on your device, are never sent to us, and are deleted when you clear the app’s data or uninstall it.

Advertising (Google AdMob)

The apps use the Google Mobile Ads SDK (AdMob) to show banner ads, which keeps the apps free. To serve ads, measure their performance and prevent fraud, Google may automatically collect and process:

  • Device identifiers, such as the Android advertising ID and app set ID
  • Your IP address, which may be used to estimate your general location
  • Device and app information, such as device model, operating system version and app version
  • Interactions with ads, such as views and taps, and diagnostic data such as crash logs

This information is collected by Google, not by BinaryTuts, and we do not receive information that identifies you. Google’s use of this data is described in How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services and the Google Privacy Policy.

You can reset or delete your advertising ID, or opt out of personalized ads, in your phone’s settings (usually Settings → Google → Ads or Settings → Privacy → Ads). You can also manage ad personalization at My Ad Center.

Permissions

The apps request only the Internet permission, which is used to load ads. Links to outside websites open in your web browser, where that website’s own privacy policy applies.

Sharing and selling of data

We do not sell personal information and we do not share it with anyone, because we do not collect it. The only data processing by a third party is the advertising described above.

Children

The apps are educational tools for a general audience and are not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has sent us personal information by email, contact us and we will delete it.

Emails you send us

If you contact us by email, we use your email address and message only to reply to you. We do not add you to any mailing list, and we will delete your messages on request.

Data deletion

Because we keep no personal data, there is no account or server data to delete. To remove the settings stored on your device, clear the app’s data in Settings → Apps or uninstall the app. To remove data held by Google for advertising, use the advertising ID controls above.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. Changes are posted on this page with a new “Last updated” date.
